01 / AUDIENCE & DIRECTION

先验证一种参与故事的需求，再决定做多大。Start with a reader’s need, then decide how much to build.

我希望探索 AI 陪伴领域，但不想一开始就投入大量资源。我的做法是先用可控的成本做出一个能用的版本，让用户亲自试用，再根据反馈判断是否值得继续投入。I wanted to explore AI companionship without starting with a large platform. I chose to build something small and affordable, put it in people’s hands, and see whether they wanted to keep using it.

在深入使用 Character.AI、JanitorAI，并尝试一些互动叙事产品后，我注意到一个值得探索的场景：读者想与自己喜欢的小说角色交流，却往往需要先整理人物设定，再反复复制到聊天工具中。我希望减少这些准备工作，让他们更快进入故事。After using Character.AI and JanitorAI extensively and trying other interactive story products, I focused on one situation: readers already had favorite books and characters, but had to organize and copy character details before they could start talking. I wanted to make that preparation easier.

最初面向的两类用户The two groups I started with

喜欢自定义角色的玩家People who want to shape their characters

他们希望自由设定人物性格、说话方式和故事背景，也在意角色能否在长时间对话中保持一致。They want to choose a character’s personality, voice, and background—and have those traits hold up as the conversation continues.

读完小说仍意犹未尽的读者Readers who are not ready for a story to end

他们想继续与书中角色交流，亲自参与剧情，或为一个令人遗憾的结局写下另一种可能。They want one more conversation with a character, a place in the plot, or a chance to rewrite an ending.

准备上架，让我重新思考产品的方向。Preparing for release made me rethink its purpose.

最初，我想做一款以自由对话为核心的 AI 角色陪伴应用。基本功能完成后，我开始为首次提交 App Store 做准备，并研究内容安全与年龄分级要求。这让我意识到，开放的聊天体验也意味着更多内容处理责任。作为独立开发者，我需要考虑自己能够持续承担多大的开发和运营工作量。The original idea was an AI character companion for open-ended conversation. After the basic app was working, I studied content-safety and age-rating requirements before its first App Store submission. More open-ended conversation meant more content problems to handle. As an independent developer, I needed to be clear about what I could take on.

最初的设想The original idea

与喜欢的角色自由对话Talk freely with favorite characters

以陪伴和互动为主，让用户自由展开话题。The focus was companionship and interaction, with conversations able to go in many directions.

→
调整后的方向The direction I chose

通过角色互动，共同创作故事Create stories through character dialogue

保留角色互动的乐趣，帮助用户推敲台词、推进剧情，并通过旁白引导故事走向。Keep character interaction and use it to try dialogue, develop a plot, or narrate what should happen next.

我选择转向小说共创，是因为已有的角色设定、对话和旁白功能，都能为这一场景提供价值。随后，我围绕创作用途调整了产品名称、介绍和付费权益说明。同时，我也清楚，定位调整不能代替内容管理，应用实际提供的功能仍需与对外承诺一致。I chose fiction co-writing because the character settings, conversation, and narrative controls I had built could serve that specific purpose. I changed the name, description, and explanation of paid features accordingly. A new name would not solve content problems; the app still had to match what it promised.

转向之后，也有新的挑战What this choice costs

聚焦创作，可能会失去一部分只想轻松聊天的用户；而认真写故事的人，又会对人物一致性和前文记忆提出更高要求。这个方向能否让用户持续使用，还需要通过后续试用来判断。It may appeal less to people who only want casual conversation. People writing stories will also expect characters to stay consistent and the AI to remember earlier events. I still need to learn whether this more focused direction gives them a reason to return.

02 / PRODUCT CHOICES

把有限的投入，用在用户最先感受到的价值上。Spend limited resources on the value people can feel first.

01

让用户免费体验剧情控制Let people change the plot before paying

我曾考虑将导演模式作为付费功能，最终决定保留免费。用户只需加入一句“主角突然跌入山洞”，就能让故事朝新的方向发展。我认为，这种主动参与剧情的乐趣是产品的核心价值，用户应该在付费前就能体验。I considered charging for Director Mode, then kept it free. A user can add “the protagonist suddenly falls into a cave” and send the story in a new direction. That is a central part of the experience, so people should be able to try it before paying.

付费版则主要提供更多角色名额，以及角色资料的导入导出功能。这些工具是否足以让用户愿意付费，仍有待验证。Paid features instead focus on more characters and importing or exporting character files. Whether people will pay for those tools is still an open question.

02

由用户选择并连接 AI 服务Let users choose their own AI service

长时间对话会持续产生 AI 使用费用。我选择让用户连接自己的 AI 服务，并自行承担相应费用。这样既能控制产品早期的运营成本，也为用户保留了选择 AI 服务的自由。Long conversations create ongoing costs. I chose to let users connect an AI service they pay for, rather than fund every generated reply myself. This keeps early costs manageable and lets users choose the AI they prefer.

代价是增加了设置步骤，可能让部分新用户放弃。因此，我提供了无需配置即可体验的演示，让用户先了解产品，再决定是否继续使用。The extra setup may put new users off. A demo lets them understand the experience before deciding whether to connect a service.

03

先做好文字互动，暂缓扩展功能Start with text interaction, not a large platform

我放弃了搭建角色分享社区的构想，也暂缓了对话中实时生成图片的功能。社区需要持续处理内容与版权问题；图片生成则会增加配置步骤，也可能打断对话节奏。我希望先确认，角色互动和剧情参与本身是否具有足够的吸引力。I dropped the proposed character-sharing community and set aside real-time images in chat. A community would require ongoing content and copyright work. Images would add setup and could interrupt conversation. First, I wanted to see whether characters and stories were compelling on their own.

虽然减少了社区传播的机会和视觉玩法，但也让我能更快交付可供试用的版本。That means fewer sharing opportunities and visual features, but makes it possible to get a usable version into people’s hands sooner.

03 / SYSTEM DESIGN

用技术支持创作过程，也解释它的限制。Support the creative process—and make its limits clear.

我希望把准备角色、与角色对话和改变剧情连成一个过程。实现方式也服从这个目标：资料主要留在设备上，用户选择 AI 服务，应用负责组织内容和交互。I wanted character preparation, conversation, and plot control to feel like one process. The implementation follows that goal: data stays primarily on the device, users choose an AI service, and the app organizes the material and interaction.

01准备小说与角色Prepare books & characters
02组织本轮内容Compose the turn
03连接 AI 生成回复Request an AI reply
04展示、停止与继续Read, stop & continue

导演旁白先记录为故事事件，在下一次提问时交给 AI 参考。A director note is recorded as a story event and included with the next question.

减少准备工作Reduce preparation

从小说中整理人物设定，减少手动复制。自动提取角色也会用到外部 AI，不能把它描述为完全离线完成。Extract character details to reduce copying. Automatic extraction also calls external AI; it is not an entirely offline operation.

把控制权留给用户Keep the user in control

让用户加入旁白、停止回复或重新生成。对话未完成时保留已收到的内容，并按过滤规则处理，让人看得懂发生了什么。Let people add narration, stop replies, and regenerate. Keep received text when interrupted, subject to filtering, and make the outcome understandable.

清楚说明数据去向Explain where data goes

本地保存资料有助于减少自建服务的成本。生成和提取所需文本仍会发给用户选择的 AI 服务，隐私承诺必须说明这一点。Local storage reduces the need for owned services. Text needed for generation and extraction still goes to the chosen AI service; privacy claims must say so.

开发中的一次取舍：移除朗读功能A decision during development: removing read-aloud

系统语音原本用于增强代入感，实际试听却显得生硬。更自然的声音需要额外费用和数据传输，因此我决定先移除朗读，把精力放回文字互动。System speech was meant to improve immersion, but sounded stiff in use. More natural voices required extra cost and data sharing, so I removed read-aloud and returned attention to text interaction.

04 / PRICING CHOICES

用户已经在付 AI 费用，应用还应该为什么收费？If users already pay for AI, what should the app charge for?

让用户自选 AI 服务，解决了早期持续承担生成费用的问题，但也意味着应用需要证明自己的独立价值。我选择免费提供角色对话与导演模式，让用户先体验参与故事的乐趣。Letting users choose their own AI service limits ongoing generation costs, but means the app needs to offer value of its own. Character conversation and Director Mode stay free so users can experience participating in a story first.

付费版围绕更多角色名额与角色资料导入导出设计，计划采用 $6.99 一次买断。我的判断是，工具费用与 AI 使用费用应当分别讲清；是否愿意购买，仍需要真实用户验证。Pro centers on more character slots and character-file import and export, with a $6.99 one-time purchase hypothesis. Tool pricing and AI usage fees should be explained separately; willingness to pay still needs testing.

免费体验负责让用户理解价值；收费是否成立，要看用户是否真正需要这些工具。The free experience should make the value understandable. The paid offer has to earn its place through a real need.

05 / FEEDBACK & NEXT QUESTIONS

一次试用提供了方向，还需要更多用户来验证。One trial offers a direction, not a market verdict.

一位朋友在开发测试版中导入了一部玄幻小说，提取配角设定后，直接进入了与该角色的对话。随后，他通过导演模式加入了一句旁白：“主角突然跌入山洞。”从角色准备到剧情干预，整个过程都在应用内完成。A friend imported a fantasy novel into a development build, had the app extract a supporting character, and started talking to that character. He then added a narration: “the protagonist suddenly falls into a cave.” From preparing the character to changing the scene, it all happened inside the app.

“比自己在网页上复制粘贴设定快太多了，旁白控制很有玩桌游跑团的感觉。”“Much faster than copying and pasting character settings on the web. Directing the scene feels like playing a tabletop RPG.”
匿名朋友 · 开发测试版试用 · 经授权引用Anonymous friend · Development-build trial · Shared with permission; translated from Chinese

这次反馈让我看到两个值得继续验证的价值：减少角色设定的准备工作，以及让用户主动参与剧情。不过，这仍然只是一次朋友试用，尚不足以判断更多用户是否会持续使用或付费。This gave me two things to investigate further: easier character setup and the appeal of actively changing a story. It is still feedback from one friend, not evidence that other people will keep using or pay for the app.

接下来要验证什么What to learn next

用户能否顺利开始，并愿意继续使用？Will people keep using it after setup?

我计划邀请目标用户从演示开始体验，观察他们能否完成 AI 服务配置、开始自己的对话，以及是否愿意继续推进故事。重点了解哪些步骤让人困惑，以及用户为何中途放弃。Invite intended users to start with the demo, connect an AI service, and begin a real conversation. Observe whether they want to write another scene, where they get stuck, and why they leave.

哪些功能值得用户花 $6.99 购买？What would make it worth $6.99?

当用户需要创建更多角色或导出角色资料时，我想了解他们是否愿意一次性购买付费版。由于 AI 服务费用需要另行支付，这些工具本身能否提供足够的付费价值，是我需要验证的问题。When people need more characters or want to export character files, see whether they choose a one-time purchase. AI service fees are separate, so the question is whether these tools are worth paying for on their own.

以上是后续验证计划，目前尚无使用或购买数据。第一轮将邀请用户参与试用，并在征得同意后，通过观察和访谈收集反馈。These are next steps; there are no usage or purchase-rate results yet. The first round will use consented observation sessions and interviews.
